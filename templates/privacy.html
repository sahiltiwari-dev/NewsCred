Privacy

EvidenceCompass doesn't ask you to sign up. Here is what is stored and what leaves the app when you run a check.

What we store

  • A random visitor ID in a cookie, so your recent checks can be shown back to you.
  • The claim text, score and result of each check, linked to that visitor ID.
  • Share snapshots you create. They are public to anyone with the link and are deleted after 30 days.

What we send to other services

  • Keywords from your claim are sent to news search services to find coverage.
  • If you add a source link, EvidenceCompass fetches that page to read its title.

Your controls

Open History on the home page and choose Clear history to delete your saved checks.
